Q: What is the minimum attendance required at Anna University?
A: Under Regulation 2021, students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered course to be eligible to write the End-Semester Examination. Attendance between 65% and 75% may be condoned in genuine medical cases with a medical certificate and college approval. Below 65% attendance, a student is not permitted to write the semester exam and must repeat that semester.

Q: What attendance grade appears if I don't meet the requirement?
A: A student who falls short of the required attendance in a course is awarded the grade "SA" (Shortage of Attendance). This bars the student from writing the End-Semester Examination for that specific course, regardless of academic performance.

Q: What is the passing mark for a subject at Anna University?
A: A student must score a minimum of 50 marks out of 100 in the combined total of internal assessment and end-semester examination to pass a subject. Scoring below 50 results in a "U" grade, meaning the student must clear the subject through a supplementary (arrear) examination.

Q: What is the grading scale used at Anna University?
A: Anna University uses a 10-point absolute grading scale with letter grades O, A+, A, B+, B, C, U (fail), SA (shortage of attendance), and RA (reappearance/withheld). Under Regulation 2021, "C" is the minimum passing grade, carrying 5 grade points.

Q: How is CGPA converted to a percentage at Anna University?
A: The official formula is Percentage = CGPA × 10. For example, a CGPA of 8.5 corresponds to 85%, and a CGPA of 7.2 corresponds to 72%. This formula is applied to cumulative CGPA, not individual semester GPA.

Q: What CGPA is needed for First Class or Distinction?
A: A CGPA of 6.50 or above, with all courses passed within the permitted duration, qualifies for First Class. A CGPA of 8.50 or above, with all courses passed in the first attempt and within the permitted duration, qualifies for First Class with Distinction.

Q: What is the maximum number of credits I can register for in a semester?
A: The maximum number of credits a student can register for in a single semester is 36, not counting reappearance (arrear) or improvement courses.

Q: How do I apply for a photocopy of my answer script?
A: Students can apply for a photocopy of their evaluated answer script through their college's examination cell before the deadline announced by the Controller of Examinations. There is a fee per script (historically around ₹300, though this can change — confirm the current fee with your exam cell).

Q: How does the revaluation process work?
A: A student must first obtain a photocopy of the answer script and check it for errors such as totaling mistakes or unevaluated questions. If a discrepancy is found, a faculty member from the college who taught the subject reviews the photocopy and can recommend the script for revaluation. The student then applies for revaluation through the college, with a separate fee per script (historically around ₹400).

Q: What happens if I fail a subject?
A: A failed subject (grade "U") must be cleared by registering for and passing that subject in a subsequent Arrear/Supplementary Examination, offered when the university conducts exams for that semester's courses.

Q: How many years do I have to complete my degree?
A: For a B.E./B.Tech. program, students must pass all courses within a specified maximum duration from their first year of admission (commonly referred to as "N+2" years, where N is the normal duration of the program), including any authorized break of study.

Q: What is an authorized break of study?
A: A break of study is a university-approved pause in a student's academic program, typically for medical or other genuine reasons, which is counted within the total duration allowed for completing the degree.
